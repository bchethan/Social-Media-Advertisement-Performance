# 📊 Social Media Advertisement Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the performance of social media advertising campaigns using **MySQL Workbench** for data analysis and **Power BI** for interactive dashboard visualization. The goal is to identify high-performing campaigns, audience segments, and marketing opportunities through data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze campaign performance using SQL.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Track key marketing KPIs.
- Identify high-performing audience segments.
- Generate actionable business insights.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **MySQL Workbench** – Data cleaning and SQL analysis
- **SQL** – Data extraction and transformation
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive visualizations for:

- 📈 Total Impressions
- 🖱️ Total Clicks
- 🎯 Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- 💰 Advertising Spend
- 📦 Purchases
- 📊 Return on Investment (ROI)
- 👥 Audience Demographics
- 📅 Campaign Performance Over Time
- 📱 Platform-wise Performance

## 🔍 Business Insights

### 👥 Customer Demographics

- Users aged **25–34** account for **42.20%** of all purchases.
- Users aged **18–24** contribute **31.71%**.
- Together, these age groups generate **approximately 74%** of total purchases.

**Recommendation:** Focus marketing campaigns on users aged **18–34**, as they represent the highest-value customer segment.

---

### 🌍 Geographic Performance

- The **United States**, **United Kingdom**, and **Canada** together contribute approximately **50%** of all purchases.
- The **United States** leads with **33%** of total purchases.

**Recommendation:** Allocate a larger share of the advertising budget to these high-performing markets while exploring optimization opportunities in lower-performing regions.

---

### 💰 Underperforming Campaigns

Despite receiving some of the highest budgets, the following campaigns generated relatively few purchases.

| Campaign | Budget | Purchases |
|----------|---------:|----------:|
| Campaign_35_Launch | 71,626.83 | 11 |
| Campaign_50_Summer | 47,274.70 | 8 |
| Campaign_36_Q3 | 58,801.99 | 10 |

**Recommendation:** Review audience targeting, ad creatives, and bidding strategies before investing further in similar campaigns.

---

### 🎯 Top Performing Advertisements

The following advertisements achieved the highest conversion rates (**9%+**) in the dataset:

| Ad ID | Conversion Rate |
|-------:|----------------:|
| 189 | 9%+ |
| 154 | 9%+ |
| 190 | 9%+ |
| 56 | 9%+ |
| 179 | 9%+ |

**Recommendation:** Analyze these advertisements to identify successful creative elements and targeting strategies that can be replicated.

---

### ❤️ High-Performing Interest Segments

Users interested in the following categories generated the highest purchase volumes:

- 🎨 Art
- 💻 Technology
- 🎮 Gaming
- ✈️ Travel

**Recommendation:** Create audience-specific campaigns for these interest groups to improve engagement and conversions.

---

## 📌 Key Takeaways

- Users aged **18–34** account for nearly **74%** of all purchases.
- The **United States** is the strongest-performing market, contributing **33%** of purchases.
- Three high-budget campaigns delivered relatively low purchase volumes, indicating opportunities for optimization.
- **Ad IDs 189, 154, 190, 56, and 179** achieved the highest conversion rates (**9%+**).
- **Art, Technology, Gaming, and Travel** are the top-performing audience interests.
